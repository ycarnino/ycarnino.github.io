About

I'm a robotics student at Michigan who likes owning a problem from the hardware up to the software that makes it move.

My story starts in Brazil, which is why the map on the home page sends you here from there.

I found robotics in high school in Saline, Michigan, on FRC Team 5066, Saline Singularity. I spent all four years on the mechanical and design team, and as a senior I led both the mechanical group and the team.

At the University of Michigan I study robotics with a minor in electrical engineering, and I graduate in May 2027. I'm president of ARV, a student team of more than 40 engineers building an autonomous vehicle, and I work in the ARCAD Lab on ARCADIAN, an open-source humanoid meant to cost under $7,000.

Two summers in industry pulled me toward the electrical side: embedded machine learning for train brakes at CEIT in Spain, then antennas and test automation at Milwaukee Tool.

Outside engineering, I'm president of Phi Iota Alpha Fraternity, Inc. at Michigan.
